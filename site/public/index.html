What it is

  • Keeps selling offlineSales are saved on the phone and upload by themselves when the signal comes back.
  • Cash, Venmo and ZelleRecords how each sale was paid and works out the change. The money itself goes the way it always does.
  • Stock that counts downEvery sale takes its items off your stock. Restock from the till, or import your spreadsheet.
  • Deals and discountsBundle deals apply by themselves; add a percent or dollar discount at checkout.
  • Several registers at onceTwo or more phones can sell at the same show; stock stays shared.
  • Totals for every showBy payment, by register and best sellers, with a spreadsheet download.
  • Your own addressyourshop.venstock.org, with invite-only logins and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app.
  • We only look when you need helpVenstock staff can open your shop read-only, for at most 30 minutes, with a reason, and you see every visit.

How it works

  1. Pay the setup fee and tell us your shop's name.
  2. We set up your shop at yourshop.venstock.org and send you an invite link.
  3. Set your password and authenticator code, then add your items or import a spreadsheet.
  4. Open it on your phone at the show and start selling.
