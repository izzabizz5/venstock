How it works

Six steps from signing up to your first sale.

  1. Pay the setup feeTell us your shop's name when you pay.
  2. Get your shopWe set up your shop at yourshop.venstock.org and send you an invite link.
  3. Add your itemsSet your password and authenticator code, then add your items one by one or import a spreadsheet.
  4. Set up showsEnter the shows you're attending. Venstock will attribute sales to the show their sold at.
  5. SellOpen your dashboard and start the show. Signal or not, every sale is saved.
  6. Track salesSee real-time inventory updates and sales reports from previous shows.
